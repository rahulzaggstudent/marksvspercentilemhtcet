Model methodology: The 2025 pooled marks→percentile curve is used as the base. Each 2026 shift receives a difficulty adjustment (δ) derived from (shift avg − overall 2026 avg of 90.8) × 0.18. A harder shift (lower avg) shifts the curve rightward — same marks maps to higher percentile. Median score is cross-weighted to fine-tune the curve shape. Confidence intervals are ±0.8–2.5%ile depending on marks range. This is a prediction only; official Mahapareeksha percentiles use their own normalisation.
